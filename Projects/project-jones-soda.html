Jones Soda Poster

Different Soda In Our Time

View Original Site

Background:

The clients are individuals passionate about unique sodas, including their designs and production methods. They are soda enthusiasts exploring a range of flavors, from traditional to rare. Known for their distinctive choices, they stand out by trying unconventional soda brands like Jones Soda.

Problem Statement:

Both soda enthusiasts and casual drinkers interested in different soda types and flavors are searching for a website that can fulfill their needs. Jones Soda required an advertisement poster to raise awareness of their brand, emphasizing their uniqueness and encouraging people to try their products.

Process:

The research showed me how people enjoy trying different things in life, like various soda flavors and brands. Based on this, I focused on Jones Soda, a well-known company notable for using sugar cane in their products. Linking this insight to Jones Soda helped me understand how to design an advertisement poster aimed at increasing awareness of the brand. My goal was not only to promote Jones Soda's presence but also to highlight what makes them unique—such as their design, a specific flavor, and more.

Result:

The soda enthusiasts and regular soda drinkers encountered a different kind of advertisement through posters that allowed them to learn about Jones Soda, recognize that they are unique, and try their soda. These experiences also helped spread the word about the brand.
