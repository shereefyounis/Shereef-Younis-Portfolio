Jones Soda Poster

Different Soda In Our Time

View Original Site

Background:

This project was created as a promotional poster inspired by Jones Soda, a brand known for breaking away from traditional soda advertising through bold flavors, unconventional branding, and a strong sense of individuality. Unlike many beverage companies that focus on polished corporate marketing, Jones Soda built its identity around creativity, self-expression, and standing out from the crowd. That rebellious and playful energy became the foundation for this design project.

Rather than creating a standard product advertisement, I wanted the poster to feel expressive and visually loud in a way that matched the personality of the brand itself. The concept focused on turning the soda bottle into the center of attention while surrounding it with energetic visuals that reinforced the idea of being different. The overall composition was designed to feel vibrant, youthful, and slightly unconventional — reflecting the same offbeat personality that has helped Jones Soda stand apart within the beverage industry.

Problem Statement:

Most beverage advertisements follow a predictable formula: clean product shots, polished branding, and messaging designed to appeal to the widest audience possible. While effective, these designs often lack personality and emotional identity, making it difficult for brands to create a memorable connection with consumers.

For this project, the challenge was to design a poster that captured attention immediately while still communicating the unique spirit of Jones Soda. The design needed to feel energetic and visually engaging without becoming chaotic or difficult to read. It also needed to reflect the brand's unconventional identity in a way that felt authentic rather than overly commercial. The goal was to create something that not only advertised the product, but also represented the mindset and culture associated with the brand itself.

Process:

I began by researching Jones Soda's official branding and visual style, paying close attention to how the company uses photography, typography, and flavor presentation to create a distinct identity. One of the most interesting aspects of the brand was its focus on individuality and consumer connection, especially through its use of unconventional visuals and community-driven aesthetics. That research influenced both the tone and composition of the final design.

The poster was built around creating strong visual movement and immediate product focus. I positioned the soda bottle as the centerpiece of the composition and used exaggerated scale, floating bubbles, and layered shadows to create a more dynamic and energetic scene. The tilted angle of the bottle was intentionally chosen to make the layout feel less static and more alive, almost as if the product was moving through the space rather than simply being displayed.

Typography also played a major role in reinforcing the tone of the design. Large, bold lettering was used to create impact and help establish the rebellious personality associated with the brand. The slogan “Be different. Do Jones.” was designed to feel less like a traditional marketing line and more like an invitation to embrace individuality. Color selection focused on soft gradients contrasted with darker typography and shadows, helping the bright blue soda remain the visual focal point throughout the composition.

Throughout the process, I focused on balancing playful experimentation with visual clarity so the design could remain eye-catching without losing readability or structure.

Result:

The final result is a visually driven promotional poster that captures the unconventional personality of Jones Soda while presenting the product in a bold and memorable way. The composition creates strong visual energy through movement, contrast, and scale, allowing the bottle to immediately command attention while supporting graphics reinforce the playful identity of the brand.

The project successfully communicates more than just the product itself — it communicates attitude. Instead of relying on a polished corporate aesthetic, the design embraces individuality and creative expression, reflecting the culture and personality that make Jones Soda recognizable.

More importantly, the project demonstrates my ability to combine branding, typography, composition, and storytelling into a cohesive visual experience. By translating the brand's rebellious identity into an engaging poster design, the final piece becomes more than an advertisement — it becomes a visual statement centered around originality and self-expression.
