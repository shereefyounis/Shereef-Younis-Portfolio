Fangamer Wireframe

Gaming Merchandise For All Fans

View Original Site View Prototype

Background:

This project was created as a reimagined e-commerce experience inspired by Fangamer, a brand recognized for transforming indie game culture into collectible merchandise that fans genuinely connect with. Rather than approaching the site as a standard online storefront, I wanted the experience to feel like stepping into a space built specifically for gamers and collectors. The goal was to capture the excitement of discovering limited-edition products, plushies, apparel, and physical game releases while presenting everything through a cleaner and more modern interface. By combining bold promotional layouts with straightforward navigation, the project focuses on creating a storefront that feels energetic, memorable, and community-centered instead of purely transactional.

Problem Statement:

Many merchandise websites struggle to balance personality with usability. While gaming brands often have strong visual identities, their storefronts can become cluttered with competing content, inconsistent layouts, and checkout flows that interrupt the excitement of shopping. For fans searching for collectibles or exclusive releases, too much visual noise can make the experience frustrating rather than engaging. ​​​

This project aimed to solve that issue by designing an interface that keeps the energy and identity of gaming culture intact while simplifying the overall shopping experience. The challenge was creating a system where featured products immediately stand out, users can move naturally between browsing and purchasing, and the checkout experience feels fast, organized, and trustworthy without losing the playful personality associated with Fangamer-inspired branding.

Process:

I started by studying the structure and merchandising strategy of Fangamer's official website, paying close attention to how featured products, collections, and limited releases are prioritized throughout the browsing experience. One of the biggest takeaways was how strongly the platform relies on emotional appeal — products are presented less like inventory and more like collectibles tied to memories, fandoms, and gaming culture.​​​​​​

Using that insight, I mapped out a user flow focused on creating a smoother progression from discovery to checkout. The homepage was designed around large promotional sections that immediately direct attention toward highlighted products while smaller supporting cards introduce additional merchandise and upcoming releases. I wanted users to feel encouraged to explore without being overwhelmed by too much information at once.

For the cart and checkout experience, I focused on reducing friction and increasing clarity. Product details, pricing, quantity controls, and checkout actions were intentionally spaced and organized to make each step easy to scan and understand. The checkout layout separates customer information, shipping details, payment information, and order summaries into distinct visual sections, helping users process information quickly while maintaining confidence during purchase. The final confirmation screen was kept intentionally minimal to create a clear and satisfying ending to the shopping flow.

Throughout the design process, I focused on blending strong visual hierarchy with fan-focused branding to create an experience that feels both polished and approachable.

Result:

The final product is a Fangamer-inspired storefront concept that combines the excitement of gaming merchandise culture with the structure and usability of a modern e-commerce experience. The design creates a stronger sense of focus around featured products while simplifying the path from browsing to checkout. Large promotional areas help establish visual impact, while the streamlined purchasing flow improves readability and reduces unnecessary complexity for users. ​​​​​​

More importantly, the project demonstrates how thoughtful UX decisions can strengthen emotional engagement within an online shopping experience. Instead of feeling like a generic retail platform, the final design feels tailored toward fans who are excited about the products they are discovering. By combining clean interface design with community-driven branding, the experience transforms online shopping into something that feels more immersive, intentional, and connected to gaming culture.
