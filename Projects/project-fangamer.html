Fangamer Wireframe

Gaming Merchandise For All Fans

View Original Site View Prototype

Background:

The clients are individuals interested in all kinds of video game merchandise, including shirts, plush toys, and more. They enjoy browsing and purchasing items that catch their interest, mainly video game-related products. Known for having diverse interests and engaging in various activities and jobs, they share a common passion for video games.

Problem Statement:

People interested in gaming research and exploring various sites to find gaming merchandise often visit Fangamer. The Fangamer website required some updates to enhance its user experience, making it easier for visitors to browse and potentially make a purchase. ​​​​​​

Process:

The research provided insights into the types of merchandise gaming sites that attract people interested in video games. It also revealed how these sites organize their products, helping me understand which layouts are more appealing to display to customers. This knowledge guided me in planning the site's layout, navigation, and other details, starting with a low-fidelity prototype that marked the initial phase. The design included multiple pages to guide the customer's journey, from the home page to a congregation page for purchasing. These visuals aimed not only to solve a problem but also to tell the story of the journey and enhance its presentation. ​​​​

Result:

The gaming community at Fangamer experienced a different layout from low fidelity, which would provide better information, leading to improved navigation and a more user-friendly experience for customers interested in gaming merchandise and more through Fangamer.
