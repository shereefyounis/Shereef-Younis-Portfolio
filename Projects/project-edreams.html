Final Design

Background:

This project was designed as a reimagined user experience inspired by eDreams, a global travel platform built around helping users search, compare, and book flights, hotels, and travel packages in one place. Instead of focusing only on the booking process itself, I wanted this project to explore the moments that happen before the journey begins — the feeling of anticipation, trust, and excitement users experience when preparing for travel.

The concept centered around redesigning the login and account-access experience, an area that is often overlooked in travel platforms despite being one of the first interactions users have with a service. My goal was to create an experience that felt welcoming, easy to follow, and visually structured while still aligning with the professional and fast-paced nature of online travel booking. By simplifying the login journey and improving information clarity, the design aims to reduce friction and help users transition smoothly into planning their trip.

Problem Statement:

Travel platforms often prioritize booking functionality while treating login and account access as purely technical steps. As a result, users can encounter interfaces that feel crowded, confusing, or disconnected from the rest of the experience. Something as simple as signing in can become frustrating when users are presented with unclear pathways, overwhelming forms, or a lack of feedback throughout the process.

For this project, the challenge was to redesign the login flow in a way that felt more intuitive, supportive, and user-focused. The experience needed to guide users naturally from account access to confirmation without unnecessary complexity or confusion. At the same time, the design still needed to maintain the credibility and professionalism expected from a travel platform where users trust the service with personal information, bookings, and payments.

Process:

I began by researching eDreams' official platform and analyzing how travel services structure their navigation systems, account flows, and booking experiences. One of the key observations was how important clarity and reassurance are within travel-based interfaces. Users are often managing schedules, pricing, and travel details simultaneously, which makes a simple and predictable user flow especially important.

Using those insights, I mapped out a streamlined login journey focused on reducing hesitation and improving guidance throughout the process. The user flow was broken into three main stages: entering the login area, providing account information, and receiving a successful confirmation before continuing into the platform. Each stage was intentionally designed to feel progressive and easy to understand so users always know where they are within the experience.

The wireframes focused heavily on layout hierarchy and communication. Navigation remained visible throughout the process to preserve orientation and consistency, while the login sections were simplified to prioritize only the most necessary actions and information. Return buttons and continuation prompts were added strategically to support flexibility and user control, preventing the experience from feeling restrictive or overwhelming.

For the final interface design, I emphasized readability, spacing, and confirmation messaging to create a more reassuring experience. The success screen was intentionally designed to feel encouraging rather than purely functional, reinforcing that the user completed the process correctly while also providing direct access to account settings and next steps. Every design decision aimed to make the interaction feel smoother, more human, and less mechanical.

Result:

The final result is a refined login experience that transforms a routine account-access process into a clearer and more engaging user journey. Instead of treating login as a disconnected utility feature, the redesign integrates it into the broader travel experience by emphasizing guidance, feedback, and usability from start to finish.

The project successfully improves visual organization, reduces unnecessary complexity, and creates a more approachable flow for users navigating the platform. Clear progression between screens, structured information hierarchy, and supportive messaging help users move through the process with greater confidence and less friction.

Beyond the interface itself, the project demonstrates my ability to apply UX thinking to smaller but highly important interactions that directly influence how users perceive a product. By focusing on clarity, reassurance, and flow, the redesign creates a more polished and user-centered experience that supports the excitement and momentum of planning a trip rather than interrupting it.
