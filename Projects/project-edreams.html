Background:

The eDreams website is a travel booking platform that allows users to search and book flights, hotels, and other travel services. The website aims to provide a seamless and user-friendly experience for travelers looking to plan their trips.

Problem Statement:

Users sometimes need more options after signing in successfully, such as a quick choice to continue or access settings to make changes if needed. Providing users with more freedom and easier access helps them have a smoother experience, as a lack of access could deter them from using the site.

Try creating a wireframe that illustrates this scenario based on the travel website eDreams. With multiple types of wireframes, choose one and bring it to life with text, colors, and more to showcase it.

Process:

The process involved researching and analyzing the existing eDreams website. Then identifying areas for improvement like the login process. Finally, creating mutiple different types wireframes with the user flow chart to create an wireframe that enhance user experience and usability. After narrowing down the options, the final wireframe was created.

Result:

The wireframes successfully illustrated the proposed improvements for the eDreams website, providing a clear visual representation of the enhanced user experience and usability. Demonstrating the effectiveness of wireframing in the design process and highlighted the importance of user-centered design principles.
