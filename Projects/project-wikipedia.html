Wikipedia Wireframes

The Wiki That Holds All Information

View Original Site View Prototype

Background

The clients are curious individuals seeking information online to answer their questions. They research through various websites to find different answers related to their interests. As part of the general public, they engage in many online activities, all sharing the common goal of obtaining answers from diverse sources such as wikis. ​​​​​​

Problem Statement

Internet users exploring sites like Wikipedia often find the experience less engaging. The Wikipedia site requires a redesign to enhance user-friendliness and improve navigation, providing users with a better experience.​​​​​​​

Process

The research provided insights into how users understand and feel about different aspects of the site, such as navigation, design, and images. These insights helped me prioritize what was most important during the redesign. I focused on a few key pages to highlight the potential of the redesign and show how they connect. The choices were driven by research findings, guiding the development of designs and prototypes to showcase to users and stakeholders.​​​​​​​

Result

Users of Wikipedia experienced an improved interface with better visuals—text, images, and more —and increased navigation friendliness, creating a more enjoyable research journey.​​​​​​​
