Background

Sky: Children of the Light is a game that features beautiful visuals and an engaging storyline. The game has a unique art style that sets it apart from other games in the market.

Problem Statement

Create a visually appealing GIF that represents the essence of Sky: Children of the Light, capturing its unique art style and visuals using its logo.

Process

The process involved creating a series of animations with the Logo that captured the essence of Sky: Children of the Light. The animations were then compiled into a single animation for sharing and showcasing.

Result

The result is a visually appealing GIF that represents the essence of Sky: Children of the Light, capturing its unique art style and visuals using its logo. That also showcases the understanding of using the Adobe After Effects software to compile everything together.
