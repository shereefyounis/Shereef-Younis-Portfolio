About Me

Hi, I’m Shereef Younis, a UX Designer with a recent degree in Graphic Information Technology from Arizona State University.

My work explores websites, video games, product marketing, and other digital platforms, applying design psychology and visual communication to help people interact more seamlessly with technology.

My career goal is to use UX Design to solve problems, connect with audiences, and communicate ideas effectively across diverse industries.
